Introducing Swayve

Every moment a cheers moment.

A glass of wine that behaves like one. Tilt, and the wine leans with you. Swirl it. Tip too far and it goes over the rim, and what you spilled is gone until you pour another.

Light, not footage

Light is traced through the glass and the liquid the way it travels in life, so the wine carries the room behind it, turns it upside down, and burns where a lamp sits.

The cheers moment

Touch your phone to a friend's. You both feel the knock, and a heart rises from the glass — one for each pair of you, however many are round the table.

Pour them a glass

Tip your phone within a moment of the clink and your wine pours into theirs. No shared network is needed: the phones find each other directly, outdoors as readily as at home.

Five rooms, or your own

An izakaya, a garden at sunset, a city bar, a balcony over Sydney Harbour, a lantern-lit table in Xiangyang. Or hand it a photograph, and Swayve reads the light out of it.

There is nothing to score and nothing to win. It is a glass, and it behaves like one.
